# Research Opportunities 

I am currently looking for motivated students to join a range of research projects, from master’s research experiences and paid internships to PhD opportunities. Current topics are outlined below, but I also welcome new ideas—please feel free to contact me to discuss your interests.

## PhD Positions

- **Multimodal deepfake detection** in collaboration between ÉTS Montréal and AMIAD, start date as soon as possible.

## Internships
Six-month paid Master 2 internships at the AMIAD, starting in spring 2027, on the Polytechnique campus, Palaiseau; preferably for students from engineering schools in Palaiseau, or from Paris metropolitan area. 
Topics include:
- **Simulating radio communication data to improve the robustness of speech processing systems**
- **Detecting audio deepfakes using a database of signatures**
- **Investigating the robustness, detection, and identification of audio steganography and watermarking**
- **Streaming masking of personal information in speech**

Topics are not final, and may be subject to variations, please contact me directly to learn more.

## Master's level research experiences
Unpaid research experiences for a semester or a year, which can be rewarded with academic credits depending on your institution.
Topics include for Johns Hopkins University students: 
- **Language identification in crowds**
- **Conversation analysis using large language models**
- **Reverberation of audio and speech signals in open and enclosed spaces**

I welcome **research project proposals from Master 1 and Master 2 students at engineering schools in Palaiseau**. 
If you have an idea you would like to explore, please get in touch to discuss potential collaborations.
