Projects coming soon.

SPEAR

2025–2026

Funded by Amazon

Evaluation of speech-to-speech large language models in conversational settings.

PI: Laureano Moro-Velázquez.

Role: Co-supervision of students and creation of a benchmark for evaluating speech-to-speech LLMs in conversational interactions.

EDART: Enhanced Dialogue Analytics & Recognition Technology

Since 2023

Funded by the Human Language Technology Center of Excellence (HLT-COE)

Spoken conversation analysis, speaker characterization, and emotional state modeling using audio foundation models and multimodal large language models.

Role: Project supervision and development of multimodal language models for speech processing and speaker characterization.

SHADOW: Spoken Human Signal Anonymization and Disentanglement for the Obfuscation of Various Voice Attributes

2024–2027

Funded by IARPA, ARTS Program

Voice conversion for speech anonymization.

Role: Implementation of the evaluation protocol, development of models to validate anonymization, reproduction of voice conversion models as baselines, and co-supervision of students and PhD students.

ADPIE: Alzheimer's Disease Detection Using Portable Integrated Equipment

2024–2025

Funded by NIH & the National Institute on Aging (NIA)

Detection of neurodegenerative diseases using portable equipment through speech, eye movements, and handwriting.

Role: Development of handwriting-processing algorithms, creation of a shared codebase for data preprocessing and script execution across modalities, and co-supervision of students and PhD students.

BABYLON

2024–2025

Funded by the Department of Defense (DoD)

Research on unsupervised source separation in noisy and far-field conditions.

Role: Co-supervision of students.

GARD: Robustifying Defense GANs to Deception Attacks in Images, Video and Audio

2022–2024

Funded by DARPA

Improving the robustness of image, speech, and video processing algorithms against adversarial and poisoning attacks.

Role: Development of defenses against poisoning attacks using self-supervised learning systems, such as DINO, combined with clustering techniques, and co-supervision of students and PhD students.

RED: Optimization-Based Methods for Reverse Engineering of Black-Box Adversarial Attacks

2022–2024

Funded by DARPA

Reverse engineering adversarial attacks against speech processing systems.

Role: Development of attack classification models based on intrinsic attack properties, and co-supervision of students and PhD students.

FAI: A New Paradigm for the Evaluation and Training of Inclusive Automatic Speech Recognition

2022–2025

Funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF)

Study of biases in speech processing algorithms, measurement of these biases, and development of balancing solutions.

Role: Collection of open web data and co-supervision of students and PhD students.

CIFRE PhD Thesis with Orange Labs Rennes

2019–2022

Research on spoofing attacks using behavioral biometric data reconstruction and statistical alignment methods.
