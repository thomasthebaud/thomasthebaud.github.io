I served as a co-instructor for *Machine Learning for Signal Processing* for graduate and undergraduate students in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering in Fall 2025. The three-credit course met three times per week and enrolled 50 students.

The course focused on machine learning theory and algorithms for modeling, classifying, and retrieving information from complex real-world signals, including audio, speech, images, and video. Students completed Python programming assignments and a semester-long group project.
